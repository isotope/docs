# Isotope eCommerce manual

Build a shop you understand, from the first product to the first fulfilled order.

Isotope adds online selling to Contao. Contao supplies your website's pages, articles, layouts, files, and member accounts. Isotope supplies the catalog, pricing, cart, checkout, and order management. You assemble these parts into the shopping experience your business needs.

This manual assumes you are new to online shops. It explains the purpose of each major setting before showing how to use it. Examples use a fictional shop called **Trail Supply**, selling mugs, T-shirts, and packaged coffee.

## What this manual covers

- Shop planning and essential Contao concepts.
- Products, categories, attributes, variants, images, and pricing.
- Tax classes and rates, including how tax-inclusive prices behave.
- Store configurations, payment, shipping, customer accounts, and checkout.
- Orders, payment records, fulfillment, notifications, and documents.
- Practical setup recipes, launch checks, troubleshooting, and maintenance.
- Template customization, events, configuration, and extension boundaries.

## Version covered

This is the **Isotope 3 source-based manual**, checked against core revision `96240eaa65c33a51e37951fe9833d9db1bd5980d`. It describes that implementation, not every historical Isotope release. Your installed extensions can add fields, methods, and behavior.

Some source files and older help texts disagree. The [version and implementation notes](reference/version-notes.md) record important differences. A source review does not replace testing a real shop. Use the [launch checklist](guides/launch.md) on your own installation before accepting orders.
