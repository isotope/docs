# Why Isotope?

Isotope is useful when your shop is part of a content-rich Contao website and needs to share its pages, design, languages, and member accounts.

For example, Trail Supply can publish an article about coffee brewing and place a product list beside it. Product categories use Contao pages, so the shop navigation belongs to the same site structure as the rest of the website.

## What you can adapt

Product types let you give different products different fields. A T-shirt needs size and color, while coffee needs package information. Store configurations let you define currencies, address choices, and checkout methods. Content elements let you choose where lists, product details, and checkout appear.

Developers can change Twig templates and extend behavior through services, events, and registered types. This flexibility is particularly useful when standard shop layouts do not match your content or workflow.

## What to plan for

You still need hosting, a Contao installation, a design, product content, payment arrangements, and ongoing maintenance. Some capabilities need an additional extension or custom integration. Verify Isotope 3 compatibility for each extension before making it part of the project.

A payment method in the backend is not itself a merchant account. A shipping price is not a carrier booking. A product SKU is not a warehouse management system. Decide who or what carries out those business operations.

## A sensible starting point

Use the standard elements and a small catalog first. Once you understand an entire test order, adapt the presentation and add business-specific features. The [first-shop guide](../guides/first-shop.md) provides that starting point.
