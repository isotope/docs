# Getting started

An online shop lets customers choose products, place an order, and arrange payment and delivery. Someone also needs to maintain product information, check payments, answer questions, and prepare orders.

This section takes you through the initial setup. If your shop is already running and you want to add a product or handle an order, go straight to [Daily use](../usage/index.md).

## Know the three places you will work

**Contao Manager** installs and updates the website's software. You use it during installation and maintenance.

**The Contao backend** is the administration area. This is where you add products and process orders. Under **Isotope eCommerce**, you find **Products**, **Orders**, **Rules**, and **Configuration**. You also use Contao pages, articles, files, and forms.

**The frontend** is the shop your customers see. Open it in a separate browser session when testing, so you see the experience of an ordinary visitor.

A Contao **user** is a staff account for the backend. A Contao **member** is a customer account for the frontend.

## Follow the first-shop route

1. Read [Contao essentials](../concepts/contao.md) if pages, articles, and content elements are new to you.
2. Read [How a shop works](../concepts/shop.md) for products, carts, orders, and payments.
3. Meet [Trail Supply](../concepts/example-shop.md), the T-shirt shop used throughout this manual.
4. [Install Isotope](installation.md) with Contao Manager.
5. Follow [Your first shop](first-shop.md) to place one complete test order.
6. Work through [Before you open the shop](launch.md) before accepting customers.

You can keep the [glossary](../concepts/glossary.md) nearby while you work.

## Plan your real shop

For Trail Supply, the tutorial supplies the example values. For your own shop, decide:

- What you sell and which sizes, colors, or other choices customers need.
- Which countries you accept orders from and deliver to.
- Which currency and tax treatment your prices use.
- How customers pay and who checks that payment arrived.
- How you deliver or arrange pickup.
- Whether customers need an account.
- Who will check orders each day.

You do not need to configure every feature at once. Complete the T-shirt order first, then use [Shop setup](../setup/index.md) to add the features your business needs.
