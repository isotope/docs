# Getting started

An online shop lets customers choose goods or services, see their price, submit an order, and arrange payment and delivery. The website is only part of the process. Someone must also maintain product information, answer questions, check payments, and fulfill orders.

With Isotope, you decide how that process fits into a Contao website. Installation makes the tools available. It does not automatically produce a finished shop with your products, tax policy, delivery areas, or payment accounts.

## Three places you will work

**Contao Manager** installs and updates software packages. You normally visit it when installing or maintaining the website.

**The Contao backend** is the administration area. Under **Isotope eCommerce**, you manage **Products**, **Orders**, **Rules**, and **Configuration**. You also use Contao's page structure, articles, files, themes, forms, members, and Notification Center.

**The frontend** is the website your customers see. Test it in a separate browser session so an administrator's preview does not hide publishing or permission problems.

A Contao backend user is a staff account. A Contao member is a customer account. They have different purposes and permissions.

## Make a small plan

Before configuring anything, write down:

1. What you sell, including whether customers choose sizes, colors, or personalization.
2. Where you sell and deliver.
3. Which currency customers pay in.
4. How your entered prices treat tax.
5. How customers will pay and how you will confirm payment.
6. How you will ship, arrange pickup, or deliver services.
7. Whether customers need an account.
8. Who will check orders each day.

For a learning shop, start with one product, one currency, one delivery area, and an offline payment method. Add complexity after you can complete and process a test order.
