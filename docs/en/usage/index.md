# Daily use

Once the shop is configured, most work happens in **Products** and **Orders** in the Contao backend.

Use the sidebar’s three task groups below. This is a working reference for an existing shop. You do not need to complete catalog editing before processing today’s orders.

## Handle purchases

- [Orders](orders.md): review an order and move it through payment and fulfillment.
- [Returns and refunds](returns.md): handle exceptions without confusing status changes with real money movements.

Agree who checks new orders, who confirms offline payments, and who records dispatch. A successful checkout needs an operational process behind it.

## Maintain the catalog

- [Products](products.md): create, publish, categorize, and maintain items.
- [Variants](variants.md): manage size/color combinations and inherited information.
- [Prices](prices.md): use simple prices, quantity tiers, and conditional prices.
- [Pricing recipes](pricing-recipes.md): apply trade-customer, quantity, or timed offers.
- [Recommendations](related-products.md): keep related products useful.

Make changes on a small sample first when they affect many products. Confirm the public result, not just the saved backend field.

## Keep the shop running

Use [Routine checks](maintenance.md) for the regular schedule and [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md) when something fails.

### A daily routine

1. Review new orders and unresolved payments.
2. Investigate failed or long-running payments before contacting the customer.
3. Prepare eligible orders for delivery or pickup.
4. Record actual fulfillment and send the intended communication.
5. Review customer questions, returns, and catalog corrections.
6. Check reported checkout or notification errors with your maintainer.

For regular operational checks, see [Keep the shop running](maintenance.md). Your maintainer handles [technical upgrades](../developers/upgrading.md).
