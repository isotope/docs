# Keep the shop running

This is the shop owner’s routine for a working shop. Your maintainer handles [software upgrades](../developers/upgrading.md), backups, and technical monitoring. Agree who owns each responsibility before launch.

## Each working day

1. Review new orders and payments still processing.
2. Confirm actual payment receipts before releasing orders for dispatch.
3. Review failed order messages and customer reports of checkout problems.
4. Follow up on returns, incorrect addresses, and orders waiting for information.

Use [Processing orders](orders.md) for the detailed workflow.

## Regular checks

Place a representative test order at an agreed interval and after changes to payment providers, mail delivery, taxes, or shipping. Use the Trail T-shirt test case, with the expected amounts updated to your shop’s approved prices.

Keep checkout instructions, delivery notes, contact details, and product recommendations current. Review staff access when responsibilities change.

## Agree a recovery plan

Ask your maintainer to back up the complete shop and rehearse restoring it. Record who can restore service, how to reach them, and how staff will track orders or payments received during an interruption. Keep credentials in your approved secure location.

Before an update, agree when it will happen and who will check the customer journey afterwards. Use the [launch checklist](../getting-started/launch.md) again for changes that affect checkout.

## Cart cleanup and historical orders

Guest carts normally expire after 30 days through scheduled cleanup. This does not mean completed orders or documents are deleted after 30 days. Agree how historical customer and order information is handled as a separate business process.

## Catalog structure changes

Adding or correcting a product is routine work. Changing shared attributes, tax classes, or product types can affect many products. Arrange those changes with the person responsible for [shop setup](../setup/index.md), then check representative existing products.
