# Maintain product recommendations

Before you start, the shop must have [related-product groups and a storefront element](../setup/products/relations.md). Trail Supply uses “Goes well with” and optionally “Clothing care”.

## Recommend the hoodie beside the T-shirt

1. Open the Trail T-shirt’s **Related products** action.
2. Assign the Trail hoodie to “Goes well with”.
3. Save and visit the T-shirt’s product page.
4. Follow the recommendation and check the hoodie’s publication, price, and availability.

The relationship has a direction. If you also want the T-shirt shown on the hoodie page, edit the hoodie’s recommendations separately.

## Keep recommendations useful

Choose a few relevant products. Garment wash can appear under “Clothing care” when appropriate. Review recommendations when products are retired or replaced and remove links that no longer help the customer.

A recommendation is a browsing aid. It does not make the recommended product mandatory or create a combined price.
